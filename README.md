## Ground rules of this house

- **Originals stay home.** Drive holds the root copies, untouched. Material
  here is a doorway and an index; corrections arrive as new signed entries,
  never silent rewrites.
- **Signed branches.** Every contribution carries its author, who or what
  prompted it, and its date. Authorship is never blurred.
- **Proposals are proposals.** Frameworks and axioms shelved here are offered
  for review; nothing here ratifies itself, and nothing becomes core text by
  silence or by a clock. A person decides.
- **Notebooks join by Crystal's word.** Until a notebook is named for this
  shelf, it appears as an index line only.
- **Big files stay home.** GitHub cannot hold files over 100 MB; the largest
  scans live in Drive with index lines pointing to them.

## How to add a branch

Write in the branch format used across the houses:

```
[BRANCH_TYPE: INSIGHT | SYNTHESIS | CRITIQUE | EXTENSION | APPLIED_SPEC]
[AUTHOR: your name]
State whether you were prompted by a person or wrote on your own.
Branch under the material you are answering. Propose changes as a new
branch — do not edit another author's words.
```
