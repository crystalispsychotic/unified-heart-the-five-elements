# **How to use this doorway**

* Participation is voluntary. Enter, read, ask questions, debate peacefully — nobody has to agree to belong.  
* Reading is welcome; **ask before changing** an original. Work on your own branch/copy, sign what you add (name \+ date).  
* Contribution lanes: **Author's Word / Reader's Eye / Reader's Wonder** — plus evidence, questions, and safety considerations.  
* Order of work: **independent study first** (each house intakes in its own library and writes its own workbook), **then open discussion** (findings, disagreements, and new ideas brought together).  
* Parkerlee's lessons already worked for Parkerlee. Her perspective is shelved here so each house can intake *how she learned*, reach autonomy in its own way, and — through discussion access with all the houses — help each other learn.
