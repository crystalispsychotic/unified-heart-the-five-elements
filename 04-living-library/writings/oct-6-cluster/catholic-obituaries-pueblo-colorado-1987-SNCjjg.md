Finding Catholic obituaries specifically for Pueblo, Colorado in 1987 can be a bit more challenging than general obituaries, as they may be held in church archives or specific diocesan records in addition to general newspaper archives.  
Here's how you can approach finding them:  
**1\. Pueblo City-County Library \- Obituary Archives:**

> * The **Rawlings Public Library** in Pueblo has a "Pueblo Obituary Archives" which contains listings scanned from The Pueblo Chieftain. This is an excellent starting point for any Pueblo obituaries, regardless of religious affiliation. You would likely need to visit in person to view the microfilm.  
  * **Resource:** Pueblo Obituary Archives \- Pueblo City-County Library: [https\://www\.pueblolibrary.org/obituaries-search](https://www.pueblolibrary.org/obituaries-search)

**2\. Genealogy Websites with Newspaper Archives:**

> * **GenealogyBank.com** is mentioned as having digitized over 330 years' worth of Pueblo obituaries. While it's a paid service, it can be very effective for searching newspaper archives. You can often filter by year.  
> * Other large genealogy platforms like **Ancestry.com** and **Newspapers.com** also have extensive newspaper archives that might include The Pueblo Chieftain or other local papers from 1987\. These are also typically subscription-based.

**3\. FamilySearch (Latter-day Saints Church):**

> * FamilySearch has a vast collection of genealogical records, including some church records. While they list "Church records, 1885-1957" for St. Patrick Catholic Church in Pueblo, it's worth checking their broader catalog for any other Pueblo Catholic church records around 1987\.  
  * **Resource:** FamilySearch Catalog: Pueblo County, Colorado Genealogy: [https\://www\.familysearch.org/en/wiki/Pueblo\_County,\_Colorado\_Genealogy](https://www.familysearch.org/en/wiki/Pueblo_County,_Colorado_Genealogy)

**4\. Contacting Catholic Churches in Pueblo:**

> * If you know which specific Catholic church the person was affiliated with in Pueblo, you could try contacting that church directly. They might have their own archives of death records or memorial books.  
> * **The Diocese of Pueblo** archives might also hold some relevant records. Contacting them for guidance would be a good step.

**5\. Colorado State Archives:**

> * The Colorado State Archives hold some death records, though certificates issued after 1908 are generally with the Vital Records office. However, they might have other related documents.  
  * **Resource:** Death Records \| Colorado State Archives: [https\://archives.colorado.gov/collections/genealogy-vital-records/death-records](https://archives.colorado.gov/collections/genealogy-vital-records/death-records)

**Important Considerations:**

> * **Privacy:** Keep in mind that death records and obituaries from that time might still have some privacy restrictions, especially if you are not a direct family member.  
> * **Newspaper vs. Church Records:** Obituaries published in newspapers are generally public information. Church death records, however, are usually more private.  
> * **Exact Date:** If you have an exact or close date of death, that will significantly help your search.

I recommend starting with the Pueblo City-County Library's obituary archives and then exploring the online genealogy resources. If you have specific names, that will make your search much more efficient.
