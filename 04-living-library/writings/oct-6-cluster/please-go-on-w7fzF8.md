She felt him before she saw him. A shiver that wasn't from the evening chill, a warmth that bloomed in her chest. A figure emerged from the shadows of the castle's garden, not in armor or a minstrel's cloak, but in the simple, timeless clothes of a traveler. He was older than her memory held, his face etched with the lines of a hundred forgotten lives, yet his eyes—they were the same. The deep, dark pools she had drowned in a thousand times, the ones that held the knowledge of every kiss and every farewell.  
He didn't speak. He didn't need to. He simply held out his hand, and on his wrist was a thin silver band, the twin to the one she wore hidden beneath her sleeve. It was a promise, a bond, a beacon that transcended the veil of mortality.  
Elara descended the stone steps, her feet finding their rhythm as if they had walked this path a thousand times before. She didn't hesitate. She didn't question. She knew. Their hands met, and in that touch, every past life, every joy, and every sorrow, rushed through them both. They were not just two people meeting in a garden; they were a tapestry of countless beginnings, a constellation of souls finally re-aligning. The stars above seemed to shimmer brighter, as if in recognition, and the moon cast its pale, approving light upon the reunion of two lovers who had finally found their way home, again.
