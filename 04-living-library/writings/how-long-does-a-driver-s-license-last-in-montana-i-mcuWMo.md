In Montana, the expiration period for a driver's license varies depending on the age of the driver.

> * **Ages 21 to 67:** A standard Montana driver's license is valid for 8 years.  
> * **Ages 68 and older:** The renewal period decreases for drivers in this age range. For example, a driver who is 68 renews for 7 years, a driver who is 69 renews for 6 years, and so on.  
> * **Age 75 and older:** Drivers in this age group must renew their licenses every 4 years.  
> * **Under 21:** A license issued to a person under the age of 21 will expire on their 21st birthday.
