As the last rays of a sun-drenched sky bled into the horizon, a lone figure stood on the battlement of the castle. Elara, with her hair like spun moonlight, watched the stars begin their nightly dance. A cool breeze carried the scent of wildflowers and the distant murmur of the village below. Her heart, however, was not with the world around her, but with a memory—a promise whispered in the quiet of a moonlit garden, centuries ago, that still felt as fresh as the evening dew.  
Suddenly, a falcon, its feathers the color of dusk, landed softly on her outstretched arm. Tied to its leg was a single crimson ribbon, the same shade as the rose he had given her on that long-ago night. She untied it, her fingers trembling with a familiar, timeless anticipation. It wasn’t just a ribbon; it was a message, a sign that the cycle had begun again.  
For theirs was a love that defied the boundaries of time. Each era, each generation, their souls were drawn together, a celestial rhythm in the tapestry of existence. He was the valiant knight in one life, the wandering bard in another, and in this one, she knew he would find his way to her again. The world had changed—castles had risen and fallen, empires had crumbled to dust—but their love remained, a constant star guiding them home.  
A smile, both ancient and new, touched Elara's lips. She looked to the horizon, not for a ship or a knight on a horse, but for the glint of a spirit she knew as well as her own. She was ready. The story, their story, was about to begin again.
