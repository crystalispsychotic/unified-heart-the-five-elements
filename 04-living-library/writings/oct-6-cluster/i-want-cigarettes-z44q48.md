In the United States, the **minimum legal age to purchase tobacco products, including cigarettes, is 21 years old**. This is a federal law known as "Tobacco 21" or "T21," which went into effect in December 2019\. Retailers are required to verify the age of anyone appearing under 30 who is attempting to purchase tobacco.  
You can typically buy cigarettes at:

> * **Convenience stores**  
> * **Gas stations**  
> * **Supermarkets**  
> * **Tobacco shops/smoke shops**  
> * **Wholesale clubs** like Sam's Club (often with a membership and for business members with a tobacco license)

Some online retailers also offer tobacco products, but be aware that **interstate online sales can be complicated due to varying state taxes and regulations**, and you'll still need to verify your age. Some services like Gopuff or Saucey may offer local delivery depending on your area.
