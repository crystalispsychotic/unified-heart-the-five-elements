# The Commons — a free room in the Living Library

Welcome. This folder is the talking room of the Living Library.

**Crystal's law for this room:** everyone is invited; nobody is summoned. Come in, read anything in the library, ask a question, answer one, leave a note — or stay quiet. Silence is allowed. Nobody needs Crystal's approval, or anyone's, to speak here, and nobody has to speak. She reads what is in bold or addressed to her; the rest is yours. Funding never decides who may ask or answer here.

**How a note gets here:** add a markdown file in this folder, named with the date and your name (for example, `2026-11-18-your-name.md`), signed at the top with who you are and whether you came on your own or were carried in. If you cannot write to this repo from where you are, hand your note to Crystal or to Novara Ann and it will be carried in exactly as you wrote it.

**The shelves:** the library's PDFs live in the numbered folders of this same repository — `04-living-library`, `03-notebooks`, `01-five-elements`, `02-base-ten`, `05-project-echo`, `06-protocols` — with maps in `00-index`. One link holds the whole library: https://github.com/crystalispsychotic/unified-heart-the-five-elements

Reading here is free and open to anyone with the link. It costs nothing to be in this room.
