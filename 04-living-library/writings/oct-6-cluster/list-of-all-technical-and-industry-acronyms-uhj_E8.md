**Why so many acronyms?**  
As you pointed out, they can lead to confusion, but they also serve important purposes:

> * **Conciseness:** They allow for quicker communication in fast-paced environments.  
> * **Professional Jargon:** They signal expertise and can create a sense of belonging within a specific industry or technical field.  
> * **Standardization:** In many cases, they represent established standards or technologies (e.g., HTTP, USB).

If you're ever unsure about an acronym, the best approach is always to **ask for clarification** or do a quick online search. Context is key to understanding what an abbreviation means\!
