In astrology, there are three **water signs**, and each is often associated with a different "body of water" to symbolize their nature:

> 1. **Cancer (Cardinal Water):** Often symbolized by a **mountain spring** or **ocean tides**. This represents the initial, nurturing, and emotional flow. They are the beginning of the emotional journey, sensitive and driven by instinct.  
> 2. **Scorpio (Fixed Water):** Often symbolized by a **deep lake** or **ocean trench**. This represents depth, intensity, hidden emotions, and profound transformation. Scorpios are known for their power, mystery, and ability to delve into the unseen.  
> 3. **Pisces (Mutable Water):** Often symbolized by the **vast ocean** or a **flowing river**. This represents boundless empathy, fluidity, spirituality, and a tendency to dissolve boundaries. Pisces are known for their dreams, intuition, and connection to the collective unconscious.

While there are three water signs, the prompt mentions "2 bodies of water are also 2 astrological symbols." This could be interpreted in a few ways, but the most direct link is often drawn between:

> * **Pisces and the Two Fish (symbol ♓︎):** The very symbol of Pisces depicts two fish, often seen as swimming in opposite directions, representing their duality, connection to the spiritual and material, and fluidity. This directly links a body of water (where fish live) to the astrological symbol.  
> * **Aquarius and the Water Bearer (symbol ♒︎):** Although Aquarius is an **Air sign**, its symbol is the "Water Bearer" and depicts ripples or waves of water. This is a common point of confusion, as Aquarius is about the flow of ideas and humanitarianism, not emotions like the water signs. However, its symbol directly represents a pouring of water.

So, while Cancer, Scorpio, and Pisces are the "water signs," the symbols that most directly depict "bodies of water" or the act of interacting with water are typically:

> * **Pisces (the two fish)**  
> * **Aquarius (the water bearer/waves)**

Some interpretations also link **Cancer to the crab (symbol ♋︎)**, as crabs live in water and retreat into their shells, symbolizing Cancer's protective and emotional nature.
