In a cozy little town nestled between a sun-drenched valley and a whispering forest, lived a little girl named Lily and her mom, Cora.  
Lily had a special name she gave her mom: "Shadowsun." Not because her mom was a shadow, but because she was always there—a warm, bright sun during the day, and a comforting presence in the quiet shadows of the night.  
Cora, in turn, called Lily her "Starling." Not because Lily was a bird, but because she was a beautiful, singing star who brought light and joy wherever she went.  
One afternoon, Shadowsun and Starling were playing a game. They were building a magnificent castle out of pillows and blankets, complete with a moat of colorful scarves and a drawbridge made from a striped towel.  
"We need a dragon\!" cried Starling, her eyes wide with excitement.  
"A dragon?" asked Shadowsun with a grin. "A kind dragon or a fire-breathing one?"  
"A friendly dragon," Starling decided. "A very big, very friendly dragon who wants to live in our castle."  
They found a big, green, cuddly stuffed toy and placed him at the top of the pillow-fortress.  
"He needs a name," said Starling, thinking hard.  
"What do you think of Sparky?" asked Shadowsun.  
"Sparky the dragon\!" Starling cheered. "And he'll guard our castle and our dreams while we sleep."  
And as the sun began to set, casting long, soft shadows across the room, Shadowsun and Starling curled up inside their pillow fortress, safe and sound with their new friend, Sparky the dragon. It was their own little world, and in it, they were the happiest mother and daughter in all the land.
